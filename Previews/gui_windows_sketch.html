★ · OUR RULES

  • Never sell power. Robux buys luck, time, skips and looks. Floors, weapons' strength and leaderboards stay earned.
  • Always show the odds on anything random, with your luck included.
  • No fake "was" prices. A sale is only shown when it's real, and says how long it lasts.
  • No offers stuck on the play screen. An offer shows once, at a good moment (after your first boss, when you run out of tokens), then lives in the shop.
  • Title bar colour = what the window is: blue quests, teal bag, pink Arcade, gold shop, red boss, green rewards, purple Index. Weapons always wear their rarity colour.
  • Menus blur and dim the world and hide the HUD; low graphics turns the blur off for weak phones.
