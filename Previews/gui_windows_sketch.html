★ · THE RULES THAT KEEP IT CONSISTENT

  • Title bar colour = what the window is. Blue quests, teal bag, pink Spire and Arcade, gold shop, red boss or warning, green good news. On weapons it's always the rarity colour.
  • Same parts everywhere. Thick dark outline, hard drop shadow, pixel font for titles and numbers, a rounder font for names and sentences.
  • _ folds, ✕ closes. Walking away from a station also closes its window, like today.
  • Fights stay clean. Only the boss bar, your stats and your abilities show. Everything else waits until the fight ends.
  • Big enough for a phone. Buttons at least as big as a thumb, and nothing important in the bottom corners where Roblox's own buttons sit.
