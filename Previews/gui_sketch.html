The rules I'd stick to

  • The middle stays clear. Nothing sits where you're aiming, apart from the lock-on corners and the brief big words.
  • The top belongs to the boss: his name, his health with phase lines, the damage you just did, and one extra meter only when his fight needs it.
  • The bottom middle belongs to you: the heart, the flask to its left, stamina to its right, and the sword ability after that. Your eyes only go to one spot.
  • Lobby things step back in a fight. Coins and power fade to about 30%, and the goal bar and big buttons hide, so the fight screen looks clean.
  • One look everywhere: the chunky outlined font, dark rounded panels, and gold for rewards. A boss's own screens (the race screen, ATTEMPT) sit on top in his style and then clear.
  • Phones: everything already scales with screen height. The touch buttons go bottom right, clear of the sword ability.
