## 1. What is each hit's core loop (minute-to-minute and session-to-session)? What makes the first minute easy?

### Takeaway
Every hit has a one-verb core loop you can learn in under a minute: plant, buy/steal, survive the night, dress up. Each one stacks a slow "come back later" layer on top: crops growing while you're offline, brainrots earning passive income, a 99-night countdown, shop restocks. Most are idle/tycoon hybrids. Money comes in passively, and a social or danger layer (stealing, monsters) adds tension.

### Inferences

A seed shop next to your plot, a conveyor of brainrots next to your base, a campfire you have to keep alive, a dressing room. Your first purchase pays off in seconds (your first crop or first brainrot starts earning).
- The session-to-session hook is offline/idle progress plus clock-based events: crops growing, passive brainrot income, shop restocks, weekly admin events. Players have a reason to come back at set times, which feeds the D1/D7/D28 retention signals Roblox now rewards.
- Hits remix each other quickly. PvB = GAG + SAB + PvZ; Escape Tsunami = brainrot collecting + a survival runner; Steal an Egg = the Steal template with new content. A proven loop with a fresh theme beats new mechanics on speed to market.

## 2. What social/PvP hooks drive sharing and retention?

### Takeaway
The biggest hits make other players part of the loop. That might be a threat (stealing in Steal a Brainrot), a team (99 Nights co-op), an audience (Dress to Impress voting, showing off rare mutated crops) or a trading economy. Roblox's algorithm now directly rewards "intentional co-play."

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot: stealing from other bases is the central mechanic, together with traps and gear for defending or stealing (bought with in-game money or Robux) — [Steal a Brainrot wiki: Gamepasses](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses); [Wikipedia via search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Naavik: these games create "fast-paced interactions where value constantly shifts." The GAG vs SAB "rivalry" drove much of Roblox's 2025 engagement — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- 99 Nights: invites players "to build camps with friends." Co-op survival with classes that suit team roles ("beginners, team players, and solo survivors") — [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7326934954); [PC Gamer classes](https://www.pcgamer.com/roblox/99-nights-in-the-forest-classes/)
- Dress to Impress: rounds are judged by peer vote. "Players and streamers spend hours socializing and competing" — [Ayala Bulldog Times](https://ayalabulldogtimes.org/11245/features/dress-to-impress-robloxs-latest-fad/)
- Trading: Steal-an-Egg clones put "[🔁 TRADING]" in their game titles, which suggests trading updates are used as a draw — [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10737231383/trading-jump-to-steal-an-egg). Third-party marketplaces sell Plants vs Brainrots and Steal a Brainrot items for real money — [FunPay](https://funpay.com/en/lots/3773/); [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/sammy-steal-a-brainrot-guide/)
- Roblox's December 2025 RFY update added an "intentional co-play" signal: users manually joining a game with friends through invites, sessions or private servers. Roblox advises creators to add invite prompts and private-server options — [DevForum announcement, 11 Dec 2025 (via search)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441); [MaxPower Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/why-roblox-s-new-discovery-algorithm-favors-games-that-bring-friends-together)
- Server-wide moments: admin abuse events hit "all servers simultaneously," so everyone shares one live moment — [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-a-brainrot-en/steal-a-brainrot-admin-abuse-schedule-explained/)

### Inferences
- Stealing creates loss aversion. You come back to protect your stuff, and you want revenge, which brings players back and makes great clips. Co-op does the same job with less conflict and is now explicitly scored by the algorithm.
- A visible rare-item economy (mutated crops, Secret brainrots) gives players something to show off and to trade, and that drives TikTok clips and real-money resale.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative data on how much stealing or co-play raises retention for these specific games.

## 3. How do they monetize? Any revenue estimates?

### Takeaway
They monetize through a stack: game passes (VIP, 2x money, luck), developer products you can buy again and again (lucky blocks, money packs, server-wide luck boosts, instant restocks/skips), gacha-like rarity tiers, limited-stock restocks and timed events. Revenue estimates run from about $1M+ a month for mature hits to $12M in a single month for Grow a Garden at its peak. Roblox's 2026 algorithm change now penalises games that lean on short-term monetization over retention.

Spend still counts as a signal (spend days, Robux spent), so the aim is spending spread over many days, not big early spikes.

## 5. What do Roblox's discovery algorithm and recommendation changes in 2025-2026 reward?

### Takeaway
Roblox shifted discovery from "clicks and short-term spend" toward long-term retention and social play. The order of changes: thumbnail personalization driven by qPTR (late 2024/2025), then the December 2025 RFY update with an intentional co-play signal and creator analytics, then the 2026 update measuring retention across D1, D2–7 and D8–28, which replaced qPTR with play-through rate plus first-play bounce rate. Roblox accepted lower near-term bookings, and brainrot-style games that spike and fade lost large amounts of traffic.

### Cited Findings
- Creator docs: the recommendation system has "explore" and "expand" phases. A spike in new users after an update leads to recommendations to more user cohorts if that cohort shows good engagement and monetization — [Roblox Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- Thumbnail personalization: every hour, thumbnails with higher qPTR for a user group get more impressions from that group. Average lift +8.5% qPTR, up to +50% for some games — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/). Over 8,000 experiences adopted it, with an average +12% qPTR (February 2025) — [Bloxy News on X](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/1890113378766704674). Later update: the system "remembers your existing winning thumbnails" — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/thumbnail-personalization-now-remembers-your-existing-winning-thumbnails/3793665)
- December 11, 2025: improved RFY released globally, plus a Home Recommendations Analytics tab. Reported signals: qPTR, play days per user (7-day), playtime per user (7-day), a spend signal, intentional co-play days and D1/D7 retention — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) (signal list via a search summary of the gmmarket and DevForum pages, [gmmarket](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/how-the-roblox-discovery-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026-myths-debunked))

### Inferences
- What to build for in late 2026: (1) a thumbnail and first minute that keep people past the click, since play-through and first-play bounce are now measured; (2) reasons to return on D2–7 and D8–28 (idle progress, weekly events, long goals like 99 nights or rebirths); (3) friend-joining features (invites, private servers, co-op roles); (4) spending spread across many days rather than aggressive early monetization.
- The 2025 "viral spike" playbook (meme skin + steal loop + heavy luck/gacha sales) still gets clicks, but it now gets throttled if 28-day retention is weak. The best-placed 2026 breakouts appear to be those with deeper loops (Animal Hospital's high rating, Anime Expeditions, and GAG2 adding risk).
- Admin abuse and weekly updates still fit the new algorithm: they create play days and co-play (friends joining for the event), not just CCU spikes.
