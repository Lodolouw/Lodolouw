# Roblox Genres & Mechanics in 2026: Trend Map and Solo-Dev Feasibility

Research date: 2026-10-05. Method note: direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy for most domains (Bloomberg, DevForum, RoWatcher, Kitsblox, Panstag), so findings below come from search-result summaries of those pages. Treat figures as "reported by source" rather than independently verified. Several SEO/aggregator sites (ejaw, kitsblox, exitlag, creation.dev, game-ace, cubix, sportskeeda roundups) are low-to-medium quality; this is flagged where it matters.

## 1. Which genres grew most in 2025-2026, and which faded?

### Takeaway
The 2025-26 growth story is "meme-collectible idle/tycoon hybrids" (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Escape Tsunami for Brainrots, Plants vs Brainrots, Steal an Egg) plus co-op survival/horror (99 Nights in the Forest), stacked on a stable base of roleplay (Brookhaven), anime RPG/combat (Blox Fruits), and Murder Mystery 2. Individual meme hits rise and fall fast (weeks to a few months) and get replaced by the next variant of the same loop. Evidence that tower defense or anime genres as a whole are "fading" is weak; specific older titles (e.g. Anime Fighting Simulator) have died, but the genres persist.

### Cited Findings

- Roblox DAU rose from ~85M (end 2024) to 144M by Q4 2025 (~69% YoY) — [RoWatcher, "Is Roblox Still Growing in 2026?"](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-roblox-still-growing-in-2026-a-data-driven-look-at-the-numbers)
- Roblox share of total PC/PlayStation/Xbox playtime went from 3.3% (2021) to 7.9% (2025), per Newzoo data — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-playtime-share-newzoo-f2p-2026/)
- Q3 2025: ~11.5B of ~14B engagement hours came from mobile, only ~2.5B from PC/console (mobile-first design matters) — [ExitLag blog](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/roblox-game-genres-and-popularity/) (secondary source)
- GameAnalytics 2026 report (500+ titles with 1M+ MAU, Aug 2025-Jul 2026): median player opens Roblox ~3x/day and spreads time/money across many games; "growth comes from capturing a larger share of what already exists" — [GameAnalytics 2026 Roblox Benchmark Report](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)

### Inferences
- "Rising" is better described at the mechanic level than genre level: the winning 2025-26 loop is collect meme characters/items → passive income → social interaction (steal, trade, show off) → frequent content drops, wrapped in whatever theme is viral (gardens, brainrots, eggs, tsunamis).
- Classic "click to gain strength, rebirth" simulators appear to have been absorbed into the idle/tycoon hybrids rather than disappearing outright.
- "Fading" anime fighters/tower defense claims should be treated cautiously: the anime aesthetic persists (Blox Fruits, Anime Defenders) while specific older formats die.

### Gaps

- No CCU data for anomaly games, troll towers, or fashion/social (Dress to Impress) in 2026 was found.

## 2. Recent solo/very small team breakouts: how fast they were built and what they share

### Takeaway
The best-documented "tiny team, fast build" case is Grow a Garden: a 16-year-old built the first version in 3-4 days, but it scaled only after a veteran studio (Splitting Point) partnered and expanded the team. Most other mega-hits were small studios (3-5 people) building over ~3 months. Pure solo devs reaching the top charts are rare and poorly documented.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden: first version built in ~3-4 days by 16-year-old "BMWLux"; Janzen "Jandel" Madsen (Splitting Point Studios) acquired partial rights and expanded the team; peak ~22.3M CCU 104 days after the 26 Mar 2025 launch — [Trib Today/AP-style report](https://www.tribtoday.com/news/business/2025/08/millions-flock-to-grow-virtual-gardens-in-viral-game-created-by-teenager/); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r); [GamesBeat Madsen interview](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- 99 Nights in the Forest: Grandma's Favourite Games, three NZ founders (Alec Kieft, Matthew Hufton, Cameron Angland) plus an artist and an animator (~5 people); built March-June 2025 (~3 months), their fastest ever versus ~6 months on earlier projects — [Roblox DevForum Creator Spotlight](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940). Kieft had prior games with 1B+ plays before this — [The Spinoff 2021](https://thespinoff.co.nz/games/07-05-2021/meet-the-new-zealander-whose-roblox-games-have-been-played-a-billion-times/)
- Steal a Brainrot: developed by SpyderSammy, owned by DoBig Studios (he is its Chief Production Officer); no build-time info found — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Escape Tsunami for Brainrots: by group "Wave of Brainrots"; criticized as "low-effort" (implying a quick build), est. ~$727k/month revenue (estimate, 81% confidence) — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9363735110/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots)
- Troll tower solo example: "Trolls can't break this tower" by MikkelDevs (appears to be an individual), live since Aug 2025 with continued updates — [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/8450472355/Trolls-cant-break-this-tower)
- Claim: "horror games have the best effort-to-impact ratio for small teams"; "a small team can ship a polished horror game or obby in weeks" — [Kitsblox](https://kitsblox.com/blog/popular-roblox-game-genres-2026) (opinion, SEO blog)
- Tooling: Roblox's new mobile-first AI "Build" tool lets people publish via text prompts without Studio; 71% of alpha users who made a game had never used Studio — [Panstag RDC 2026 summary](https://www.panstag.com/2026/09/rdc-solo-creator-guide.html) (secondary); RDC 2026 keynote — [Roblox Newsroom, RDC 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)
- Leaked/"uncopylocked" Steal a Brainrot templates circulate — [HomePage Media](https://my.homepage.net/news/steal-a-brainrot-uncopylocked-roblox) (indicates template-based cloning is common; also a legal/ToS risk)

### Inferences
- Common traits: a simple, instantly readable core loop; collect/rarity mechanics; passive income that rewards returning; social friction (stealing, trading, showing off); meme-native theming; extremely frequent updates/limited-time drops after launch.
- A solo dev can plausibly build a v1 of an idle/tycoon/troll tower/obby/+1-per-second game in 1-3 weeks; scaling a hit (live ops, servers, monetization, moderation) has historically required adding people quickly (Grow a Garden pattern).
- Small studios with prior shipped titles dominate; "first game becomes a hit" stories are rare.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 solo-dev breakout with a documented build time was found.
- No reliable data on what templates/frameworks (e.g. ProfileStore, Knit, toolbox kits) top solo devs used.

## 3. Copycat saturation: do late clones still succeed?

### Takeaway
Saturation is extreme for the steal-and-defend/brainrot loop (lawsuits, "uncopylocked" templates, Fortnite ports), yet the record shows successive variants with a *new twist* (Escape Tsunami, Plants vs Brainrots, Steal an Egg) did succeed, while pure reskins face a June 2026 algorithm change that explicitly penalizes clones and clickbait.

### Cited Findings
- Bloomberg (24 Mar 2026): "The internet is awash with knockoffs"; Spyder Games and Speedy Simulator Gaming filed at least four lawsuits against alleged imitators since Nov 2025 — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-24/roblox-s-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)
- Spyder Games sued the Fortnite map "Stealing Brainrots" as a "wholesale copy"; that map hit 540k+ concurrent players — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/22635/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-devs-sue-fortnites-stealing-brainrots-dev-over-allegedly-stolen-content); [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-internet-culture,1532127); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/fortnite/steal-the-brainrot-lawsuit)
- Brainrot genre has "fast replacement cycles — new entrants constantly cannibalize older titles" (Escape Tsunami over Steal a Brainrot in Jan 2026) — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9363735110/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots)
- June 2026 algorithm update: clickbait and "template or clone games without meaningful twists are penalized"; new metrics include play-through rate and bounce rate (% leaving within 60 seconds) — [Roblox Newsroom: Optimizing Discovery (June 2026)](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [Endsights summary](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox); [lensblox](https://lensblox.com/blog/roblox-algorithm-change-2026-explained/)
- Recommended For You moved from a 7-day to a 28-day window measuring D1, D2-7, D8-28 retention; qPTR split into play-through, session quality, and spend; new "Standout Games" curated sort with a nomination form for novel games; KreekCraft publicly credited it with reviving new games — [Roblox Newsroom June 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)
- Aug 2026 discovery update rewards games strong in both retention and monetization together — [GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)
- Discovery is increasingly age-aware: younger players favor short-form games, older players deeper games — [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)

### Inferences
- Late *identical* copycats are now a poor bet: the 28-day retention window and clone penalty work against a quick reskin that spikes then drops.
- Late copycats *with a meaningful twist on the theme or loop* remain the dominant path for small devs (every 2025-26 meme mega-hit was itself a twist on the previous one).
- Trend lifespan for a single meme hit appears to be ~2-6 months at peak (Grow a Garden Mar→~mid-late 2025; Steal a Brainrot May 2025→Jan 2026 displacement); a 1-3 week build cycle fits inside that window only if the dev spots the trend early.
- Legal risk is real for copying assets/code verbatim (lawsuits).

### Gaps
- No count of "Steal a X" clones on Roblox was found; no data on the success rate of late entrants.

## 4. Realistic expectations and advice for a new solo dev in 2026

### Takeaway
Median Roblox performance is low (D1 ~10%, sessions ~7-10 min, ~3.8% payer rate), so targets for a testable prototype are roughly D1 ≥10-15% and sessions ≥10-15 min, with hits typically at 19+ min sessions. Ads are cheap per click, but most small games lose money on ads unless revenue per visit beats cost per visit. Successful devs ship many games; expect most attempts to earn little.

### Cited Findings
**Metrics benchmarks**
- Median D1 10.3%, D7 1.6%, D30 0.5% (even for 1M+ MAU games); only 3.80% of players pay — [GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report); [Mellow/GameDevReports summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox)
- Median session 9.8 min; top 5% 16.6 min; top 1% 21.1 min; another figure: median 7.28 min, 75th percentile 14.8 min; "hits emerge at 19+ minutes" with double-digit retention — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report); [RRQ World 2026 retention benchmarks](https://blog.rrqworld.com/roblox-game-retention/) (figures differ by sample; both reported)
- Community rule of thumb: aim for at least 8 min playtime and 10% D1 — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/suggestions-to-improve-average-session-time-day-1-retention/2935525)
- Bounce rate (leave within 60 s) and play-through rate (home impressions → plays) are now explicit discovery metrics — [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-optimizing-discovery-how-great-games-reach-millions-of-players-on-roblox)

**Ads (Roblox Ads Manager)**
- Roblox (2025): cost-per-play "over 71% cheaper", players for "less than $0.01"; new flow (Mar 2025) gave a 44% CPP drop and 89% improvement in qPTR by early May 2025 — [DevForum: Leveling Up Ads Manager](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/leveling-up-ads-manager-with-new-features/3587640); [DevForum: More Ads Manager Upgrades](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-ads-manager-upgrades/3659656)
- 2026 developer estimates: ~4-12 Robux per visit depending on targeting, category competition, and thumbnail; obby and tycoon are at the high end — [RoWatcher: Roblox Ads in 2026 break-even math](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-ads-in-2026-the-break-even-math-small-devs-ignore). CONFLICT: Roblox's "<$0.01 per play" claim versus 4-12 Robux; the two are measured differently (USD ad spend versus Robux) and at different times.
- Developers can set max bid per play very low (e.g. $0.01-0.03) — [DevForum: What is Cost Per Play?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-is-cost-per-play/2908034)
- Historic complaint: "23 Robux per play" on the new Ads Manager (early 2024) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-ads-manager-23-robux-per-play/2786509)
- Break-even example: 2% payer rate × 80 Robux ARPPU = 1.6 Robux revenue per visit, far below 4-12 Robux CPV; RoWatcher states most small games lose money on ads; DevEx cited at $0.0035/Robux — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-ads-in-2026-the-break-even-math-small-devs-ignore)
- Ads for Creators RDC recap thread (Sept 2025) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)

**Attempts and expectations**
- Realistic expectation for a new, unproven game is $0 to a few dollars; only ~10% of active games see regular updates or high player counts — [generalistprogrammer](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/how-much-do-roblox-game-developers-make-earnings-data); [search summary](https://asset-sherlockbot.adobe.com/how-many-games-are-there-on-roblox) (low quality)
- Example of a portfolio developer: six games released, five reached "top ten visited", one (Parkour Tower) did not — [The Spinoff on Alec Kieft](https://thespinoff.co.nz/games/07-05-2021/meet-the-new-zealander-whose-roblox-games-have-been-played-a-billion-times/) (2021, dated)
- 99 Nights team had multiple prior titles before its breakout — [DevForum Creator Spotlight](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940)

### Inferences
- Practical launch strategy implied by the evidence: ship a small v1 in 1-3 weeks; run a small ads budget purely as a *test* (buy a few thousand plays) to measure D1, session length, bounce, and qPTR; kill or iterate fast; only scale ads if revenue per visit exceeds CPV. With the 28-day discovery window, plan at least 2-4 weeks of post-launch updates.
- Suggested go/no-go targets for a prototype (synthesized, not from one source): bounce low, D1 ≥10-15%, average session ≥10 min (≥15-19 min suggests hit potential).
- Feasibility ranking for a solo 1-3 week build (judgment): highest = troll tower/obby variants, "+1 every second", anomaly/spot-the-difference (content-light, systems-light); medium = idle grow/steal-and-defend tycoon (systems are simple but need DataStores, anti-exploit, live content drops and tuning economies); lower = co-op survival horror (AI, multiplayer state, art), fighting games (netcode/hit feel, balance), fashion/social (needs large asset libraries).

### Gaps
- DevEx rate: RoWatcher cites $0.0035/Robux; Roblox reportedly raised DevEx in Sept 2025 (the commonly reported figure is about $0.0038) but no source confirming this was retrieved here. Verify.
- No official 2026 Roblox CTR/thumbnail benchmarks were found.
- No survey data on how many attempts successful solo devs make before a hit.
