# New Breakout Roblox Games 2025-2026: Player Numbers, Growth, and Current Trends (as of early October 2026)

Method note: the web fetch tool was blocked for every source tried (Wikipedia, Rolimons, RoTrends, RoWatcher, StudioKrew, about.roblox.com). All findings below come from search-engine result snippets of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Treat exact numbers as "as reported in the snippet", and check the flagged items before relying on them. Several aggregator blogs contain obvious errors (for example, one says Grow a Garden was "released in 2026"; it launched in March 2025), so single-source numbers are marked.

## 1. Which 2025-2026 launches reached the highest CCU / fastest growth?

### Takeaway
The 2025 class (Steal a Brainrot at 25.8M CCU, Grow a Garden at about 22M, 99 Nights in the Forest at 14.2M, Plants vs Brainrots at 8.65M) set all-time records for any video game. The 2026 class has two clear breakouts: Steal An Egg (launched July 2026; trackers report a 14.27M peak on Sept 19, 2026, but this is not confirmed by mainstream press) and Escape Tsunami for Brainrots (Dec 2025, about 5M peak). Grow a Garden 2, Animal Hospital and The Forge are mid-tier hits, each peaking around 0.4-1M.

## 3. Which 2025 hits have faded, and how fast?

### Takeaway
The mega-hits fade quickly once the novelty passes. Steal a Brainrot fell from a 25.8M peak (Oct 2025) to about 176K (Aug 30, 2026). Grow a Garden fell from about 22M (Aug 2025) to about 63K (Sept 2026). Grow a Garden 2 went from about 970K (June 2026) to a few thousand by mid-September 2026 according to one tracker. Typical lifespan from launch to peak is 2-5 months, followed by a 95-99% drawdown within about 12 months.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot: launched May 2025, peaked Oct 2025 (5 months), 176,229 CCU on Aug 30, 2026, which is about 0.7% of peak and about 75% below its own average of 880K — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- RoWatcher characterises brainrot titles' CCU as "event-driven spikes followed by steep declines" — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- Grow a Garden: launched March 2025, peaked August 2025 (5 months), about 63K in September 2026 (about 0.3% of peak) — [BloxQuiz](https://www.bloxquiz.gg/stats/grow-a-garden). The original still held about 140K at GAG2's launch in May 2026 — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78614-grow-a-garden-2-has-launched-on-roblox-here-s-everything-new-added-in-this-sequel)
- Grow a Garden 2: peak about 972K on Jun 18 (4 weeks after launch), about 454-484K in mid-August, then 6.1K on Sept 11, 2026 (down 91.5% over 30 days) per one tracker. Conflicting, see above — [RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/game/97598239454123); [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/)
- 99 Nights in the Forest: 14.2M peak, about 169K CCU "as of 2026" (about 1.2% of peak) — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/99-nights-in-the-forest-vs-steal-a-brainrot)
- Plants vs Brainrots: 8.65M peak in Oct 2025; "downfall" coverage. No current number found — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HCSqu8Vt8bY)
- Older evergreen games such as Murder Mystery 2 (all-time peak of 1.35M in Aug 2026) and Brookhaven RP (86.6B visits, ~567K daily peak in Aug 2026) outlast the trend games — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/)

### Inferences
- Trend games reach the 1M+ CCU tier in days to weeks and peak within 1-5 months. They tend to keep a long tail of 50K-200K CCU, which is still a top-50 game on Roblox. Sequels (GAG2) do not reliably inherit the original's scale.
- Event spikes such as admin abuse and concerts can briefly restore millions of CCU to a declining game (Steal a Brainrot had 12.8M for a Bruno Mars concert in Jan 2026, after peaking in Oct 2025).

### Gaps
- No precise early-October 2026 CCU for any of the faded titles. The nearest datapoints are from late August to mid-September.

## 4. Who made them: solo developers vs studios, and quick builds?

### Takeaway
The biggest hits often started as solo or very-small-team projects that were quickly taken on or co-owned by experienced Roblox studios: Grow a Garden (a 16-year-old solo dev, built in about 3 days) and Steal a Brainrot (solo creator SpyderSammy, owned by DoBig Studios). Professional and esports-linked organisations (Misfits Gaming with Steal An Egg, SuperGaming with Showdown) increasingly drive 2026 launches. Earnings are extremely concentrated.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden: built by 16-year-old BMWLux "in like three days." Jandel (Splitting Point Studios, New Zealand) and DoBig Studios came in as co-owners and scaled it with live updates — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/a-roblox-farming-game-made-by-a-teenager-in-like-three-days-had-8-9-million-players-online-at-the-same-time-steam-peaked-at-11-5-million-across-all-games-on-the-same-day/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Steal a Brainrot: developed by SpyderSammy (BRAZILIAN_SPYDER group), owned by DoBig Studios — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Roblox fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/BRAZILIAN_SPYDER/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- 99 Nights in the Forest: Grandma's Favourite Games, a small New Zealand group with 3 named founders (founded 2020) — [Roblox fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Grandma's_Favourite_Games); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/)
- Steal An Egg: Misfits Gaming portfolio ("another breakout success across the Misfits games portfolio") — [Misfits on X](https://x.com/MisfitsGG/status/2090785862632038706)
- DoBig Studios appears as a co-owner on both Steal a Brainrot and Grow a Garden. This points to a small set of studio-investors behind multiple mega-hits — [HandWiki](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Software:Grow_a_Garden); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Creator economics: Roblox's top 10 creators averaged $65.7M each, while the median creator made $1,500 — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-dollars65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-dollars1500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html)

### Inferences
- A common pattern: a quick, simple prototype by a solo or teen developer catches on, then an established studio partners or buys in, adding live-ops (weekly updates, admin events) and marketing that scale it to millions. Rapid live updates and event-driven spikes appear more decisive than initial production value.
